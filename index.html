FAQ

Common questions

What is Firm Garage?

An independent directory that helps traders research and compare proprietary trading firms — account options, rules, profit splits, and payout policies in one place.

What is a prop trading firm?

A firm that provides eligible traders with access to trading capital under specific rules and conditions. Depending on the firm, you may need to complete an evaluation or qualify for a particular account type.

How do I choose the right firm?

Compare account prices, drawdown limits, profit targets, payout conditions, and trading restrictions. Choose rules that match your strategy and risk tolerance.

Does Firm Garage sell funded accounts?

No. We're a research directory. To purchase an account, visit the firm's official website directly.

Are rules and prices always up to date?

Prop firms change their terms without notice. Always verify the latest details on the firm's own website before making a purchase.

Is Firm Garage affiliated with the firms listed?

Not necessarily. A listing does not imply endorsement or partnership. We may earn affiliate commissions from some links.
