Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about FundedEdge and prop firms.

What is FundedEdge?
FundedEdge is an independent directory that helps traders research and compare proprietary trading firms, their account options, trading rules, profit splits, and payout policies.
What is a prop trading firm?
A proprietary trading firm provides eligible traders with access to trading capital under specific rules and conditions. Depending on the firm, traders may need to complete an evaluation or qualify for a particular account type.
How do I choose the right prop firm?
Compare account prices, drawdown limits, profit targets, payout conditions, trading restrictions, and the firm's reputation. Choose rules that suit your trading strategy and risk management.
Does FundedEdge sell funded accounts?
FundedEdge is a research and comparison directory. Visit the relevant firm's official website to review its current account options and purchase conditions.
Are prop firm rules and prices always up to date?
Prop firms can change their prices, rules, and payout policies. Always verify the latest terms on the firm's official website before making a purchase.
Does FundedEdge guarantee payouts or profits?
No. FundedEdge does not guarantee trading profits, account approval, or payouts. Trading involves risk, and each firm's terms and eligibility requirements apply.
How can I visit a prop firm's website?
Browse the directory, select a firm, and use its official website link where available. Check the firm's own website to confirm its rules, pricing, and eligibility requirements.
Is FundedEdge affiliated with every listed firm?
FundedEdge is intended as an independent research directory. A listing alone does not mean that a firm endorses or is affiliated with FundedEdge.
