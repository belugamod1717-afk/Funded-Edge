Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about prop firms and Funded Edge.

What is a proprietary trading firm?

A proprietary trading firm (prop firm) provides traders with capital to trade financial markets. Instead of risking their own money, traders use the firm's capital and split the profits.

Is Funded Edge a prop firm?

No. Funded Edge is an independent research and comparison tool. We do not provide trading capital. We help you compare and find the best prop firm for your needs.

How do you rank the firms in the directory?

We rank firms based on a combination of factors including user reviews, profit splits, drawdown limits, pricing, and transparency. We do not accept payment for higher rankings.

What does "Max Drawdown" mean?

Max Drawdown is the maximum amount of your account balance you are allowed to lose before the account is closed. It's a crucial metric to check before buying a challenge.

Are the prices shown accurate?

Prices are updated regularly, but they can change. We always recommend clicking "View Firm" to check the official website for the latest pricing and rules.

How do I choose the right prop firm?

Consider your account size, budget, profit target, and the firm's drawdown rules. Use our directory filters to narrow down your options based on these criteria.
